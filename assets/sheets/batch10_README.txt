OPERATION: LOST FRONTIER — BATCH 10
ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS & EFFECTS

New categories in this batch:
- Water effects: splashes, waves, waterfalls, ripples and droplets
- Lava and fire: lava pools, eruptions, fire columns and flame bursts
- Ice and snow: icicles, ice spikes, snow clouds and frost bursts
- Explosions: blast bursts, smoke clouds and ground impact rings
- Electric and energy: lightning arcs, energy fields and electric impacts
- Toxic hazards: poison clouds, slime pools and toxic spills
- Wind and air: whirlwinds, gusts and dust swirls
- Ground hazards: cracked earth, falling rocks and spike eruptions
- Mechanical hazards: spike plates, saws, crushers and laser barriers
- Special/environment/weather effects: portals, rain, clouds, storms and sun

FILE NOTE:
This ZIP contains one composite PNG atlas and this README. It is not a
collection of separately cropped transparent PNGs. The checkerboard and
section labels are baked into the generated atlas. Crop individual effects
and clean their backgrounds before importing into Unity. For animated
effects, create frame sequences and configure timing in Unity.
