OPERATION: LOST FRONTIER — BATCH 04

Contains two high-resolution master sprite atlases.

Atlas 1: Character and enemy variations, weapons, ammunition, gadgets, power-ups, effects, vehicles, terrain, props, and HUD.
Atlas 2: Character pose/animation references, enemies and bosses, weaponry, pickups, visual effects, UI panels, and biome background thumbnails.

IMPORTANT: These are combined master sheets, not individually sliced sprites. The checkerboard background is baked into the artwork and may need removal. Slice the atlases into individual sprites and verify frame dimensions/pivots before importing into Unity.
